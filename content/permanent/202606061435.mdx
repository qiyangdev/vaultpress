Whether a note system can keep working over the long term depends first on whether the friction of capture is low enough.

If capturing an idea requires too many steps, the idea may disappear before it enters the system. A [fleeting note](../fleeting/202606061425.mdx) should therefore be allowed to be rough, repetitive, and incomplete. The cleanup stage can decide what is worth preserving.
