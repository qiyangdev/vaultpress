title: Literature notes are a form of translation
description: Permanent note example about literature notes as translation
tags:
  - permanent
---
The value of a literature note is not in preserving the original wording, but in translating external material into language I can reuse.

This translation has at least three layers:

1. What the author is really trying to say.
2. How I understand the idea.
3. What problem the idea can help explain.
