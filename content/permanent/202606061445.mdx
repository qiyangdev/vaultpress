---
title: Permanent notes should stay atomic
description: Permanent note example about atomic notes
tags:
  - permanent
---
A permanent note should ideally carry one clear idea.

When a note tries to explain too many things at once, it becomes harder to link, harder to reuse, and harder to grow. Being atomic does not mean being short. It means the note has a clear central claim.
