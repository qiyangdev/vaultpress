A thought: what determines whether a note system can last may not be the perfection of its categories, but whether the friction of capturing ideas is low enough.

* Write it down first, without worrying about the perfect name.
* Decide later that day or week whether it deserves cleanup.
* Turn valuable material into a [permanent note](../permanent/202606061435.mdx).
