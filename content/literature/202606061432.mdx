## Key Notes

The Zettelkasten method emphasizes producing small, clear ideas continuously, rather than piling materials into a large classification system.

## My Understanding

A literature note is not just a record of what I have read. It is a form of translation: turning the author's argument into language that my future self can still understand and use.
